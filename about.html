About GPS

A packaging company in Kumasi built for growing businesses

Ghana Packaging Solutions helps startups, food vendors, restaurants, caterers, retail shops, event planners, families, and small businesses in Kumasi and across Ghana access affordable, reliable packaging that improves presentation and supports sales.

Practical packaging with a brand mindset

Packaging is often the first physical experience customers have with your business. We help you choose packaging that protects your product, presents it well, and gives customers confidence before they even open it.

From everyday food packaging Kumasi businesses need to event packaging for funerals, weddings, birthdays, and community gatherings, GPS focuses on simple advice, consistent supply, and fair pricing.
