About GPS

A packaging company in Kumasi built for businesses, brands, and events

Ghana Packaging Solutions helps startups, pharmacies, hospitals, food vendors, restaurants, caterers, retail shops, event planners, families, and small businesses in Kumasi and across Ghana access affordable, reliable packaging that improves presentation, protects products, and supports sales.

Practical packaging with a brand mindset

Packaging is often the first physical experience customers have with your business, pharmacy, hospital, food brand, or event. We help you choose packaging that protects your product, presents it well, and gives customers confidence before they even open it.

From everyday food packaging Kumasi businesses need to custom paper bags for local pharmacies, hospital packaging supplies, bottles for startup brands, plain and custom eatery disposables, and event packaging for funerals, weddings, birthdays, and community gatherings, GPS focuses on simple advice, consistent supply, and fair pricing.
